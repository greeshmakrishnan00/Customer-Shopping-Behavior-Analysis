## 📌 Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project developed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, product preferences, spending behavior, and customer demographics.

The project combines **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to transform customer shopping data into meaningful business insights.

Python was used for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, SQL was used for business-focused analysis, and Power BI was used to develop an interactive dashboard for KPI monitoring and visualization.

The objective is to understand customer behavior and provide data-driven insights that can support marketing, sales, customer retention, and business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering important business questions such as:

- Which product categories perform best?
- What are the major customer purchasing patterns?
- Which customer segments contribute more to sales?
- How does customer demographics influence purchasing behavior?
- What is the impact of discounts on customer purchases?
- How do subscribed and non-subscribed customers differ?
- Which payment methods are most preferred?
- What are the customer spending patterns?
- Which customers can be considered high-value customers?
- What strategies can improve customer engagement and retention?
